# NERC CIP Overview

## Purpose

NERC CIP stands for North American Electric Reliability Corporation Critical Infrastructure Protection.

NERC CIP standards establish cybersecurity requirements for organizations that operate or support the North American Bulk Electric System (BES).

The standards are designed to reduce cybersecurity risks that could affect the reliable operation of the bulk power system.

## Important Scope

NERC CIP applies to qualifying organizations and assets associated with the North American Bulk Electric System.

It is not a general cybersecurity standard for every organization.

For this laboratory:

- The Ubuntu system is not a Bulk Electric System asset.
- The simulated PLC and HMI are not NERC CIP-covered BES Cyber Systems.
- The lab therefore demonstrates NERC CIP concepts for educational purposes.
- No NERC CIP compliance or certification is being claimed.

## Key NERC CIP Areas

### CIP-002 - BES Cyber System Categorization

CIP-002 focuses on identifying and categorizing BES Cyber Systems based on their impact on the reliable operation of the Bulk Electric System.

Important activities include:

- Identifying applicable BES Cyber Systems.
- Determining their impact category.
- Maintaining appropriate documentation.

### CIP-003 - Security Management Controls

CIP-003 addresses security management controls.

Examples include:

- Cybersecurity policies.
- Security management responsibilities.
- Personnel awareness.
- Management oversight.

### CIP-004 - Personnel and Training

CIP-004 addresses personnel-related cybersecurity requirements.

Organizations may need controls covering:

- Personnel risk management.
- Security awareness.
- Training.
- Access authorization.
- Personnel changes.

### CIP-005 - Electronic Security Perimeter(s)

CIP-005 focuses on controlling electronic access to applicable BES Cyber Systems.

Security concepts include:

- Electronic Security Perimeters.
- Electronic Access Points.
- Remote electronic access.
- Access controls.

### CIP-006 - Physical Security of BES Cyber Systems

CIP-006 focuses on physical protection of applicable cyber assets.

Examples include:

- Physical access controls.
- Physical security monitoring.
- Protection of critical cyber assets.

### CIP-007 - System Security Management

CIP-007 addresses security controls for applicable BES Cyber Systems.

Examples include:

- Security patches.
- Malicious code prevention.
- Security event monitoring.
- System access controls.
- Ports and services management.

### CIP-008 - Incident Reporting and Response Planning

CIP-008 addresses cybersecurity incident response.

Organizations establish processes to:

- Identify cybersecurity incidents.
- Report qualifying incidents.
- Respond to incidents.
- Maintain incident response plans.
- Review and improve response procedures.

### CIP-009 - Recovery Plans

CIP-009 addresses recovery planning for applicable BES Cyber Systems.

Recovery activities may include:

- Recovery procedures.
- Backup considerations.
- Restoration processes.
- Testing recovery plans.
- Updating recovery documentation.

## Compliance Lifecycle

A simplified NERC CIP compliance lifecycle can be represented as:

1. Identify applicable assets.
2. Categorize applicable cyber systems.
3. Establish cybersecurity controls.
4. Protect electronic and physical access.
5. Monitor security events.
6. Respond to cybersecurity incidents.
7. Recover affected systems.
8. Maintain evidence and documentation.
9. Periodically review and improve controls.

## ICS/SCADA Relevance

NERC CIP is particularly relevant to power-sector environments containing:

- Control centers.
- Generation facilities.
- Transmission facilities.
- Substations.
- Protection and control systems.
- SCADA systems.
- Industrial communication networks.

## Laboratory Applicability

The current laboratory uses an Ubuntu environment and simulated ICS/SCADA configurations.

There is no evidence that the laboratory represents a NERC CIP-covered BES Cyber System.

Therefore, NERC CIP is treated as a reference framework rather than a compliance claim.

## Key Takeaway

NERC CIP provides mandatory cybersecurity requirements for qualifying organizations and systems associated with the North American Bulk Electric System.

Its requirements demonstrate how cybersecurity governance, access control, monitoring, incident response, recovery, and documentation can be integrated into power-sector operations.
