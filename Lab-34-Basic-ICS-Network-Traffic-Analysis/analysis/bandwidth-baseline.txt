Baseline interpretation:
The controlled ICS simulation generated low-bandwidth,
repetitive TCP traffic. Communication consisted of short-lived
connections carrying small application payloads on the simulated
Modbus and DNP3 ports.

This baseline can be used for comparison with future captures.
Unexpected increases in packet rate, bandwidth, connection
frequency, or payload characteristics could indicate abnormal
activity and should be investigated.

Environment:
AWS EC2 Ubuntu Linux
Interface:
loopback (lo)

Limitation:
The measurements represent a controlled simulation rather than
a production ICS network.
